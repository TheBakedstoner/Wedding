Ankit Weds Anamika wedding invitation website

Open index.html with VS Code Live Server. Keep the assets and audio folders beside index.html.
The cover artwork is used only as a background; Ankit, weds, Anamika, and the Open the invitation button are real HTML/CSS elements.
The cover includes interactive fireworks and opens into a 3-page invitation.
The local song attempts autoplay after 2 seconds and starts at 8 seconds; browsers may block autoplay with sound until user interaction.
Upload the complete folder to Netlify to publish.
